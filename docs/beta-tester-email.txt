I'm wrapping up development on Kollab - a music collaboration platform that lets musicians work together on projects from anywhere.

What is Kollab?

It's a mobile app built for making music together:

- Create projects with metadata (title, genre, BPM, key, time signature)
- Upload audio stems - vocals, drums, bass, guitar, keys, synth, and more
- Mix tracks with volume, pan, mute, and solo controls
- Add timestamped comments directly on the audio timeline
- Real-time chat with your collaborators
- Invite collaborators with role-based permissions (viewer, contributor, editor, admin)
- Automatic BPM detection from your audio files
- Follow other musicians and discover their projects
- Showcase your work with audio highlights on your profile
